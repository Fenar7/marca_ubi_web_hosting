## Scope delivered

The CRM surfaces now use the approved Commercial Desk direction: the public site's light visual language, Circular XX typography, restrained Marca Ubi red, full-digit INR, an editorial navigation shell, and responsive operations views. Authentication and reset-password pages use the new split brand/workspace composition. Command Center and analytics use bounded, source-backed reporting, a useful attention queue, and explicit unavailable/empty states. Leads and deals retain role-scoped data and server-side pagination/search. Follow-ups use UTC queue boundaries, bounded pagination, error/retry states, and the existing atomic completion action. Works, team, inquiries, and settings have matching responsive visual treatment and avoid substituting invented record details.

Additional integrity work isolates simulated inbound records from operational reporting, rejects simulation merges into live contacts, and protects follow-up completion with transaction-level stale-timestamp checks and activity logging. The E2E login helper now requires explicit role credentials, a loopback target, and `CRM_E2E_DATABASE_ISOLATED=true` before attempting authentication.
