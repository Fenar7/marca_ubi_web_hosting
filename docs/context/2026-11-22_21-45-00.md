## 2. Problem Diagnosis & Root-Cause Analysis (Why Previous Work Failed)

During user auditing of live screenshots (`Screenshot 2026-11-20 151543.png`, `151601.png`, and the SLA Velocity Gauge widget), severe visual and structural defects were identified:

1. **The Typography Slop Defect**: Headings (`.pageTitle`, `.entityHeading`) and numeric counters (`.kpiValue`, `.scorecardValue`) were hardcoded to heavy system bold weights (`600/700`). The project possessed genuine luxury font files (`HelveticaNeue-Light.otf` at weight 300 bound to `--font-helvetica-neue`), but SCSS rules never consumed them. The resulting UI looked like generic, unstyled AI wireframes.
2. **Bento-Box Inception**: Both the Follow-Up Queue and the Lead 360 Workspace used stacked, nested rounded boxes with 3px top borders and double outlines (`.queueOverview`, `.stripeScorecard`, `.sectionCard`).
3. **AI-Slop Decorative Relics**:
   - `.titaniumDot`: An ungrounded glowing blue bullet dot rendered immediately before the title *"SLA Velocity & Delivery Health"*.
   - `.radarDot`: Pulsing animated circular radar pings next to priority text and header status pills.
   - `.statPodIconChip`: Saturated, toy-like circular icon chips (`#ecfdf5`, `#fffbeb`, `#f1f5f9`).
4. **Turnaround Telemetry Cognitive Contradiction**: In the SLA Velocity Gauge, an overdue deficit of `-31d` rendered in emerald green with an upward chevron (`▲`), creating a dangerous operational status contradiction.
5. **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)**: The loading skeleton `.loadingRow` was hardcoded to `114px`, whereas `.taskItem` was `118px`, causing a visible 4px vertical jump upon data resolution.
