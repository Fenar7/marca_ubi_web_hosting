## 2. Engine work (A: typo tolerance, B: continuity, C: ownership)

1. **`CHAT_ABBREVIATIONS`** — six closed-class shortcuts (`abt`, `th`, `u`, `ur`, `pls`, `plz`) fold to the
   word they stand for. `svc`/`mktg` are absent on purpose: they name a subject, and a subject the corpus
   does not spell out stays in the denominator.
2. **`isScaffold()`** + **`SCAFFOLD_NEIGHBOURS`** — a token one keystroke from grammar *is* grammar
   (`plese`, `wtih`). Deletion **and** transposition are indexed; substitution at distance 2 is not, which
   is where `art` starts reaching `are`. Never applied inside a word under five characters, and never to a
   word the corpus uses — `town` stays a subject although it is one edit from `down`.
3. **`residue()`** — `contentTokens` with both forgiveness rules. All-grammar messages return the whole
   message rather than an empty vector that silently matches nothing.
4. **`typoStems()`** over `vocabulary`/`stemNeighbours` — one neighbour is the word (full credit), several
   is a hint (half credit), none is charged to the visitor. The multi-token case replaces the old rule that
   only fired when both sides were a single word.
5. **Residue naming** — aliases grouped by topic words (`residueOwners`), consulted after the exact-name
   check and only when exactly one intent owns the set. `20-` §4.1's authoring rule holds: the polite
   sentence and the one-word sentence reach the same author.
6. **`coreAllowed`** — the scaffolding-free trigram comparison runs only when every topic word is one this
   corpus has used, so `weather in calicut` cannot be smoothed into the studio's own city name.
7. **`CONTINUITY_PRIOR = 1.08`** as a *second pass* in `resolve()`, gated on `FLOOR_THRESHOLD` and on
   `familyRoot()` equality. Cold-first is the safety property; the previous turn can break a tie, never
   overturn an answer.
8. **`identity.studio`** authored with 18 utterances; `what do you do` moved off `services.overview` and
   `who are you` / `about you` moved off `process.team` (whose answer is two street addresses).
   `what are you` deliberately stays with `smalltalk.areyouhuman`.

**Decision reversed mid-pass:** `lookup()` was gated on a Tier-1 hit and `MatchOutcome` gained a `tier`
field. Both reverted. A wrong detour costs a re-read; a question filed as an answer corrupts the lead.
`do you do seo` is a Tier-2 hit at 1.000 and would have been missed. The asymmetry is the argument — do
not re-litigate it.

## 3. The ten intake defects

| # | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mid-brief question filed as the slot's answer | `engine.lookup()` detour (`useChatEngine.ts:190`, `ChatPod.tsx:979`), shape-gated by `looksLikeQuestion()` on blanks |
| 2 | Bare `no` at the agency question did nothing | `polarityMatch()` (`intake/slots.ts:383`) before the skip test; two yeses → ask which |
| 3 | *Start a project* mid-brief silently refused | `whereIsTheBrief()` (`ChatPod.tsx:782`) on chip and typed routes |
| 4 | Trigger line and first question landed out of order | Ordered speech queue `speakAt`/`pendingLines` (`ChatPod.tsx:222`) |
| 5 | Stale chip tap did nothing | Tray cleared in `say()`; every refused tap goes through `answerMovedOn()` (`ChatPod.tsx:745`) |
| 6 | Detour left the tray on the wrong question's buttons | Detours answer with `chipsForSlot(slot)` |
| 7 | 16 service chips + Continue pushed the composer out | `.tray` `max-height: 7.5rem`, `overflow-y: auto`, `overscroll-behavior: contain` |
| 8 | Progress moved backwards on a correction, stalled on a skip | `Question N of 17` from the slot index while asking |
| 9 | A mistake two questions back needed a full stop | `editReturn` resume point (`useIntakeFlow.ts:58`, `207`, `314`) + `Back to <label>` chip |
| 10 | Esc during `SUBMITTING` hit a guard with no feedback | *It is mid-send, so I will not cut it off.* |

The finding generalises beyond this surface: **the machine's refusals were correct and its lines were
missing.** A silent correct decision is what a visitor reads as a dead button. Any future guard in this
pod must emit a sentence naming what it turned down and what it would accept instead.

## 5. Rules learned the hard way (do not re-litigate)

- **Never run `npx tsx -e '<imports>'`** on this box — it crashes the process. Write a scratch file; the
  two probes now live in `scripts/chat-pod/` instead of the repo root.
- **`assert.equal(condition, expected, true)` is not a test.** The third argument is a message. Two of
  these were written this session and rewrote as `assert.deepEqual`.
- **A stale test pin can be the defect.** `refuses an edit outside the review card` was a deliberate pin of
  the behaviour that made defect 9 real; deleting it was correct, not collateral damage.
- **Do not promote a contested residue.** Five intents claim `[work]`; answering them at 1.000 stops the
  pod answering the work question at all.
- **Authoring outranks arithmetic, and arithmetic outranks continuity.** Each new mechanism sits *below*
  the one before it in that order, or the pod starts guessing.
