## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Resolution

Following user critique of recycled Conyest components (the bulky 12-month dual-bar graph and the 6-color circular rainbow donut), the Commercial Desk visual cockpit was redesigned from the ground up according to the **Vellum & Linear Minimal Editorial Archetype (Option 2)**:

1. **Permanently Excised Recycled Conyest Components**:
   - Excised `ConyestCashflowCard` (bulky dual-bar chart).
   - Excised `ConyestStatisticCard` (rainbow open-arc donut chart).
   - Removed all hardcoded rainbow color arrays (`["#18181b", "#ea002a", "#d97706", "#059669", "#6366f1", "#0284c7"]`).

2. **Built Bespoke Vellum Velocity Spline (`VellumVelocitySpline.tsx`)**:
   - Continuous Fritsch-Carlson monotone cubic Hermite Bézier spline curves for Deal Intake Velocity vs. Realized Revenue.
   - Whisper-thin horizontal gridlines (`#ebebea`), soft area fill gradient (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)` and `rgba(234, 0, 42, 0.16)`), baseline anchor, and authentic Marca Ubi Red (`#ea002a`) milestone marker dot.
   - Interactive crosshair scrubber and floating obsidian telemetry card with clamped positioning.
   - Integrated Linear-style timeframe pills (`7D` / `30D` / `90D` / `QTR` / `YTD`) wired directly to server timeframe navigation with `useTransition` loading indicators.

3. **Built Bespoke Pipeline Yield Ledger (`PipelineYieldLedger.tsx`)**:
   - Horizontal multi-segment conversion track replacing the circular donut gauge.
   - WAI-ARIA tabbed switcher between Valuation (`By Value`, ₹) and Volume (`By Volume`, Deals).
   - Strict Marca Ubi luxury stage palette:
     - Slate Triage (`#71717a`)
     - Dark Charcoal (`#454542`)
     - Obsidian Primary (`#18181b`)
     - Marca Ubi Red Accent (`#ea002a`)
     - Executive Amber (`#d97706`)
     - Executive Emerald (`#059669`)
   - Sequential inter-stage drop-off indicators (`↓ XX% attrition (YY% pass-through)`).
   - Interactive stage selection wired to `selectedStageId` to isolate records across the Commercial Action Desk.

4. **Brand Assets & Typography Maintained**:
   - Authentic vector `<MarcaUbiLogo />` (full wordmark and monogram with `#ea002a` dot).
   - Canonical `circularXX` and `graphik` font tokens.
