## 1. Executive Summary

Executed a full-fledged, master-grade redesign of the Marca Ubi Commercial Desk (`/admin`) and Executive Sidebar (`AdminSidebar`), completely discarding consumer banking templates (Conyest fake credit cards, bento boxes, stalactite graphs, and rainbow donut segments) and replacing them with a Swiss Editorial Luxury standard inspired by Vellum and Linear.

### Key Architectural & Visual Transformations:
1. **Executive Telemetry Masthead (`ExecutiveTelemetryMasthead.tsx`)**:
   - Replaced credit card widget with a clean typographic telemetry strip with 4 scorecards:
     - Active Pipeline (`pipelineValue`, `pipelineCount`)
     - Converted Revenue (`convertedValue`, target realization `revenueDelta`)
     - Weighted Potential (`weightedPotential`, stage-weighted probabilities)
     - Commercial Velocity (`averageCycleDays`, cycle target comparison)
   - Integrated timeframe selector dropdown and CSV summary export.
2. **Interactive Linear Stage Velocity Flow Bar (`PipelineStageFlowBar.tsx`)**:
   - Replaced rainbow donut chart with a sequential, proportional horizontal stage progression bar.
   - Interactive stage selection filtering both deal count and deal value simultaneously.
3. **Two-Column Commercial Working Surface (`CommercialActionDesk.tsx` 70% + `CadenceUrgencyRail.tsx` 30%)**:
   - Replaced Conyest disconnected transactions table with an operational Command Desk.
   - Tabbed filtering (`[All Deals]`, `[High Value (> ₹10L)]`, `[Stalled / Follow-up]`), client search input.
   - High-density two-tier client rows with Apple HIG squircle initials avatars, stage status pills, relative overdue telemetry, and quick action buttons (`[Advance Stage →]`, `[Open Dossier ↗]`).
   - Integrated Revenue Realization Target Widget (₹15L quota tracker) and Urgent Follow-ups Queue in the 30% cadence rail.
4. **Guarded Modal & Drawer Workflows**:
   - `[Advance Stage →]` triggers `StageTransitionModal` directly from the desk for atomic CRM updates.
   - `[Open Dossier ↗]` routes or emits `open-lead-drawer` custom event to open the comprehensive lead dossier without navigation loss.
5. **Authentic Swiss Executive Sidebar (`AdminSidebar.tsx`)**:
   - Clean brand row with monogram `M.` in Circular XX + `MARCA UBI` uppercase wordmark and `⌘B` collapse trigger.
   - Purged clumsy bordered `COMMERCIAL DESK` badge and duplicate sidebar search capsule.
   - Canonical 2-tier structure (`Work` and `Workspace`) containing all 9 tabs (`command_center`, `tasks`, `leads`, `deals`, `works`, `ingestion`, `analytics`, `users`, `settings`).
   - Active nav states with Warm Alabaster `#f5f5f3` pill and 3px signature red `#ea002a` active pip.
   - Integrated 1-line cadence micro-rail with 3px hairline track, eliminating heavy boxy card.
   - Consolidated Briefly-style user profile card with role badge and logout action.
