## 1. Root Cause Forensics & Direct Fixes

### A. Sass Compilation Error in `ExecutiveKpiGrid.module.scss`
- **Root Cause:** A leftover duplicate selector block (`.detail`, `.iconBadge`, `.badgeSlate`) sat outside `.heroCard` after line 119, ending with an unmatched closing brace `}` at line 135 (reported by Next.js at line 169).
- **Remediation:** Eradicated the redundant orphan fragment and properly balanced all selector braces. Sass compilation now succeeds cleanly.

### B. Complete Eradication of the Pitch-Black Boxes ("That Black Box is Same")
- **Root Cause:** Card 1 in `ExecutiveKpiGrid.tsx` had `isHero: true` which rendered `.heroCard` with `background: linear-gradient(135deg, #09090b 0%, #151518 100%)`. Simultaneously, `AdminSidebar.tsx` rendered `.sidebarPromoCard` with `background: #09090b`. To the user, these dark high-contrast blocks made the redesign feel like the exact same wireframe with an intrusive black box.
- **Remediation:**
  1. Set `isHero: false` on Card 1 in `ExecutiveKpiGrid.tsx`.
  2. Upgraded `.heroCard` in `ExecutiveKpiGrid.module.scss` to the pure white elevated luxury scorecard archetype (`background: #ffffff; border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08);`).
  3. Now **ALL 4 KPI scorecards** are uniform, high-contrast, clean white scorecards with crisp 2.4rem tabular numerals, soft squircle icon badges, and micro-trend pills conforming 1:1 to the Amirul Islam benchmark.
  4. Redesigned `.sidebarPromoCard` in `AdminSidebar.module.scss` to an elegant, light-mode telemetry container (`background: #f7f7f8; border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08);`).
  5. Upgraded `.navItemActive` in `AdminSidebar.module.scss` to an elevated white card with subtle border and crisp 3px crimson indicator, eradicating the pitch-black active navigation background.

### C. Live Active Deals Restoration ("Recent Opportunity Data is Unavailable")
- **Root Cause:** In `src/app/admin/(workspace)/page.tsx`, `dealsStatus` evaluated `statusesError || topLeadsError || directoryError`. When `crm_team_directory` returned an error or was empty in local development, it caused the entire `CommercialDealsTable` to report data as unavailable and hide all 10 active leads.
- **Remediation:** Since `lead.assigned_to` already defensively falls back to `'Former team member'` or `'Unassigned'`, `directoryError` was decoupled from `dealsStatus`:
  ```tsx
  const dealsStatus = statusesError || topLeadsError ? 'unavailable' : 'ready';
  ```
  All 10 active commercial leads are now rendered with full fidelity.

### D. Ground-Up Layout Restructuring (Amirul Islam 2-Column Archetype)
- **Root Cause:** In the previous layout, `RevenueDynamicsCard` and `PipelineAllocationCard` dominated the top half of the screen. Because conversions are 0 in the development window, `RevenueDynamicsCard` appeared barren and empty, while the rich `CommercialDealsTable` was buried below the fold.
- **Remediation:**
  - In `src/app/admin/(workspace)/page.tsx` and `page.module.scss`, restructured the layout into `.mainGridSection`:
    - **Left / Primary Column (62% width):** `CommercialDealsTable` ("Active Pipeline Ledger / Recent Opportunities") displaying live opportunities with company names, client initials circular avatars, deal values in tabular numbers, and stage status tokens.
    - **Right / Secondary Column (38% width):** `PipelineAllocationCard` ("Pipeline Allocation / Stage Velocity") featuring the Briefly 12px segmented track and stage rows with hover elevation.
    - **Lower Sections:** `AttentionQueue` (High-priority triage with Rillo filter tabs) and `RevenueDynamicsCard` (Pacing Engine) positioned cleanly below.

### E. Elimination of Monospace AI-Slop & Developer Tags
- **Root Cause:** Uppercase developer tags (`• COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS · LIVE TELEMETRY`, `• ACTIVE PIPELINE LEDGER`, `• STAGE VELOCITY`, `• HIGH-PRIORITY TRIAGE`) and monospace table headers gave the UI an internal developer dashboard aesthetic rather than consumer-grade luxury SaaS.
- **Remediation:**
  - Converted all eyebrows to clean sentence-case `#64748b` typography with emerald pulse dots.
  - Converted all table headers in `CommercialDealsTable.tsx` to sentence-case (`Opportunity / client`, `Stage`, `Expected value`, `Account owner`, `Actions`).
  - Converted table initials avatars from square boxes to 34px circular avatars with `#f1f5f9` backgrounds and `#0f172a` bold monograms.
  - Replaced monospace fonts on trend pills, status badges, and overdue badges with clean sans-serif typography (`var(--font-admin-primary)`) with tabular numerals.
